# Ring 0

A top down arena game I made in Godot! (somewhat inspired by a game jam I recently participated in)
YOU are an antivirus. And since antiviruses don't get guns, you don't get a gun either. Instead, you get malware to battle to the death with. "What do I use to fight the malware? Why am I an antivirus? Why don't I get a gun?" None of these questions are important except the first one, to which the answer is: you use more malware. Fight fire with fire by hooking malware into a quarantine ring around you, where you can let them revolve and do damage to enemies on hit or fling them into other enemies!

Test out a very early demo [here](https://butteredsaltedtoast.itch.io/ring-0).

## Controls
- move with WASD
- throw out your hook with RIGHT CLICK
- throw the quarantined enemy closest to your aim with LEFT CLICK
- restart after you die with R

there are currently two enemy types. more on the way though (maybe next week in terra? or later, i don't know)

there's the packet, a basic enemy. they just run towards you.
there's the ddos bots, which come in a swarm of 6 starting wave 3. they are faster than you BUT can be shredded by an orbiting enemy.
